# Changelog

What’s new in TaxTrack. Plain language only.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- A bell lights when a published changelog is new, and opens the changelog.
- Terms and a privacy policy, with a sign-up warning if the recovery key is lost.
- Each section has its own colour in the bar.
- A zip backup includes the receipt and invoice files.
- Assets are rose, with a glow in that same colour.
- A chosen year's zip also holds that year's Excel and PDF.
- The boxes glow in the colour of the section you are in.
- Receipt photos are stored smaller and open large enough to read.

### Fixed

- Log off stays visible on a phone.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- Choose fixed rate or actual costs for each income year.
- A standard day is set in the menu. One field changes a single day.
- Log off sits beside the menu.

### Changed

- Expenses opens at the top of the page.
- The menu folds display, the standard day, and features into sections.
- Help explains the fixed rate and how a bill becomes an actual-cost claim.
- Help lists each topic in a sidebar that jumps to that section.
- A blank weekday reads as office. Not working marks time between jobs.
- The working-from-home switch is an even pair of buttons.
- The logo keeps its shape beside log off.
- Usual working days are set in the menu. Other days are shaded.
- A rental bill saves without a description. The category name is used.
- Adding a record shows a short confirmation. Required fields have a star.
- Log off stays on the dashboard and leaves the menu.
- Delete account and data removes the sign-in and encrypted records.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-15

### Changed

- The rental log uses the address selected above. Rename address is a separate button.
- The phone section bar gives each label its own space.
- The opening screen moves on instead of staying up.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Rental properties. Turn them on in the menu.
- Log rent received, and the usual rental expenses, for each address.
- Summary, Excel, and PDF include rentals while the switch is on.
- Depreciating assets. Purchase price, effective life, and the yearly decline in value.
- Car kilometres on Expenses, at the ATO cents per kilometre rate.
- Email sign-in is required before the summary. Records sync as encrypted text.
- Log off, in the menu, returns to the sign-in page.
- Change password from Account, while signed in.

### Changed

- Rental addresses stack in a list. Add address opens in a box.
- Pinch zoom is off. Text size in the menu still changes the type.
- Opening screen says the records are zero-knowledge encrypted.
- Sign-in opens that account's records. Later changes are stored with it.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Private tax and EOFY organiser. Records stay in this browser.
- Working-from-home hours at 70 cents. Cents disregarded.
- Expenses with a photo, PDF, or Word invoice.
- 2027–28 stays hidden until 1 July 2027.
- Excel and PDF reports, with the TaxTrack name and logo.
- Dark opening screen.
- Help, from the menu.
